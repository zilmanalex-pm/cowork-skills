# Client Intake Questionnaire — Template

Use this as the basis for generating a client intake questionnaire Word document (.docx).
When asked to create a questionnaire, read this template and generate a polished,
fillable Word document using the docx skill.

The document should feel warm and specific — not a generic corporate form.
Adapt the language to the profession where obvious, but the structure stays the same.

---

## Section 1 — Who You Are

1. Full name, as it should appear on the site
2. Credentials, licenses, or certifications relevant to your work
3. How long you have been practicing
4. What methodology, framework, or approach guides your work
5. In your own words: how do you describe yourself as a professional?
   (Not credentials — character, presence, how clients experience working with you.)
6. What drew you to this work?

---

## Section 2 — What You Do

1. Your main areas of focus or specialization
2. Types of clients you work with (individuals, businesses; relevant demographics or sectors)
3. Types of clients or situations you prefer not to take on
4. What working with you actually looks like — a first engagement, a typical session or project
5. What makes your approach different from what a client could find elsewhere

---

## Section 3 — Your Clients

1. Who is your ideal client?
   (Describe as a person — who are they, what is going on for them, what brings them to you?)
2. What do clients typically say when they first reach out — their words, not professional framing
3. What hesitations do people have before starting to work with you
4. What does a successful engagement look like at the end
5. What feedback do you get most often from clients (specifically about you)

---

## Section 4 — Your Practice

1. Where you are based (and whether location matters for remote work)
2. How you work: in-person, remote, or both
3. Languages you work in
4. How you structure engagements (sessions, retainers, projects, packages)
5. Whether to publish pricing on the site
6. How you prefer new clients to contact you
7. Whether you are currently available for new clients

---

## Section 5 — The Website

1. Primary goal of this website (attract clients / credibility / both — rank them)
2. What you want a visitor to feel after 2 minutes on your site
3. The one action you most want visitors to take
4. What would make you uncomfortable on your site
5. Websites you admire — links or descriptions

---

## Section 6 — Tone and Look

1. Up to 5 words describing the atmosphere you want the site to convey
2. Where on the spectrum from warm-and-personal to formal-and-authoritative
3. Color preferences, and any colors to avoid
4. Professional photo availability

---

## Section 7 — What You Already Have

1. An existing bio or professional description
2. Any articles, posts, or talks you have published
3. Any phrase or paragraph that felt like it truly captured your work
4. Professional photos or visual assets

---

## Section 8 — Anything Else

1. Anything important not covered above
2. Concerns or hesitations about having a public website
3. Timeline or deadlines

---

## Generating the Document

When creating the Word document from this template:
- Use the docx skill
- Match visual style to the profession (calm for wellness, authoritative for legal, energetic for coaching)
- Include answer lines after each question
- Add brief contextual hints in italics for open-ended questions
- End with a short note explaining what happens with the answers
